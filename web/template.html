How a professor gets ranked

RateMyProfessors publishes an average and a rating count, never head-to-head results, so matches are simulated. Each professor becomes a Beta posterior built from their average and how many ratings back it, pulled toward the field mean of 3.79 by a weak prior. Every match draws one sample from each professor’s posterior, the higher draw wins, and Elo updates normally. The board averages 10 independent tournaments of 500 rounds.

A 5.0 held up by five ratings therefore loses to a 4.7 backed by two hundred — the thin posterior is wide enough that it regresses toward the field. Professors with fewer than 5 ratings are not ranked at all.

How tiers are assigned

All 25 divisions reproduce VALORANT’s published population distribution by board position, not by score: the top 0.05% are Radiant, the next 0.17% Immortal 3, down to the bottom 0.57% in Iron 1. The published shares total 100.02% rather than 100, so assignment normalises by that actual total. Cumulative share crosses 50% inside Gold 2, so Gold straddles the median professor, as it does in game.

Adjacent ranks are often not distinguishable. The bar under each Elo spans one standard deviation across the tournaments. Where two bars overlap, the data does not separate those professors and the order between them is arbitrary — including across a tier boundary.
